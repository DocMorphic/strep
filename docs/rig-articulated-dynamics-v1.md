# Supplied-GLB body tracks and explicit articulated demands

`scripts/rig_body_tracks.py` samples complete supplied-GLB joint transforms into declared rigid-body COM positions and axes. `scripts/rig_articulated_dynamics.py` freezes a request, original GLB and complete method sources, then combines those tracks with the [body-tree diagnostic](articulated-motion-dynamics-v1.md). This is an offline diagnostic component; it does not edit animation, infer anatomy or calibrate gameplay stats.

A binding names an explicit body ID and existing skin-joint node, node-local COM offset, fixed node-local body orientation and provenance. Body IDs/nodes must be distinct. Arbitrary joint names and binding order are supported. Every sampled body transform must remain a proper rigid transform within the declared numerical accuracy: animated scale, reflection and shear are rejected rather than stripped by quaternion conversion. World placement rotates both COMs and body axes. The original selected clip is sampled over its full duration, including exact endpoints, on an explicit uniform 3–900 sample clock within 1–240 Hz and thirty seconds. Added uniform times are not claimed to be the original glTF keys.

The physical profile separately supplies positive segment masses, physically valid COM inertias in those body axes, parents, both connected joint anchors and provenance. Its body order must match the explicit track bindings. The workflow requires a complete external world force and torque-about-COM population for every declared body at every sample, declared gravity and support phases, numerical anchor/force/torque tolerances and notes explaining body/reaction/support/capacity assumptions. Missing reactions are not inferred from a preview. A declared zero is an explicit modeling assumption. Unrepresented character segments remain outside the model; their masses and reactions are not certified by a smaller declared tree.

The body diagnostic checks original joint anchors, interior rigid-body demands, every child-to-parent lever-arm moment, internal joint loads and necessary floating-root balance. Unknown/cross-phase stencils remain unassessed; endpoints have no derivative estimate. Passing root balance cannot approve internal actuation, joint restrictions, grip/ground allocation or human capacity. No actual character receives inferred mass, anatomy or strength from the skin or joint names.

## Immutable workflow

The complete `strep-rig-articulated-dynamics-v1` request fields are `schema`, hash-bound `glb`, `animation_index`, `bindings`, `profile`, `sample_count`, `placement`, `rigid_tolerance`, `external_forces_world_N`, `external_torques_about_com_world_Nm`, `gravity_world_m_s2`, `phases`, `anchor_tolerance_m`, `force_tolerance_N`, `torque_tolerance_Nm` and `assumption_notes`. The latter contains explicit `body_model`, `external_wrenches`, `support` and `capacity` explanations. The tests construct complete executable request examples; the two analytical bodies are synthetic fixtures, not a human physical profile. Requests/GLBs are bounded at 64/256 MiB and must stay in the project; actual runtime resources remain guarded independently.

With provisioned local dependencies and a declared profile/reaction population:

```powershell
.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/run_guarded_job.py --worker scripts/rig_articulated_dynamics.py --output reports/my-body-demand-guard --expected-rss-mib 2048 --stable-seconds 15 --admission-seconds 600 --max-seconds 3000 -- reports/my-body-request.json reports/my-body-demand
```

The fresh ignored output retains original request/GLB bytes, method snapshots, protocol hashes, complete `body-tracks.json`, `demands.json` and a final receipt. Original/copied inputs and methods are checked after measurement. Existing output cannot be overwritten; a changed input or invalid body model leaves a failed pipeline and its available raw snapshots/tracks/demands. The supplied GLB is never changed by the workflow. Each result keeps physical approval unset and quality/release/training approval false, including positive root-balance fixtures. Studio integration, a real declared humanoid profile, independent full-body numeric replay, force allocation, capacities, continuous collision/impacts and engine/human review remain separate work.

## Validation

**64 focused tests pass in 5.71 seconds**: eighteen new COM/body-axis bridge checks, eight immutable workflow checks and 38 earlier tree/rigid-body checks. Analytical fixtures cover world placement and nonidentity body axes, arbitrary-name/binding order, original clip preservation, complete derivative/end-point accounting, explicit static root reactions, remaining child loads, missing wrench populations, disconnected anchors, nonrigid animation, mismatched body order and source mutation after measurement. The first run has 63 passes and one overly narrow expected error string: the existing sampler already rejects animated scale before the bridge. The corrected fixture accepts that earlier rejection; production behavior is unchanged by the fixture repair.

All fifteen test-supervisor resource observations independently replay. Sampled peak RSS is 107,298,816 bytes. These small analytical GLB/body fixtures use their separate 512 MiB estimate plus 600 MiB reserve; full actual rig studies retain the 2 GiB estimate plus reserve. Hosted checks of the added source are separate. Continue the [same full-project goal](project-goal.md); synthetic positives and software passes do not admit human training data or supply release evidence.
