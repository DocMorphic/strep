# Between-key refinement in Studio

The native foot-support editor now exposes **Refine between-key foot contacts**
as an unchecked choice. It runs the tested [serialized corridor refinement](native-support-sampled-repair-v1.md)
with a fixed eight-iteration limit. Rig preparation remains a separate explicit
choice and may be combined with refinement. Neither option changes the saved
original contact draft or relaxes its limits.

The job snapshots its choice and budget alongside the source/draft hashes.
Review validates the fitting request against that frozen choice, the exact
original or prepared input, and the unchanged draft. Archived fitting methods,
seeds, probes and repair audits are verified before serving. Legacy jobs without
the new fields remain readable without a refinement claim.

The comparison adds starting proposals before each completed final proposal.
Per-trial entries show accepted steps, attempted refinements and the bound audit
link. It retains the existing contact and motion-rate failure feedback. Failed
or unreachable proposals remain visible; a repaired contact does not become a
quality-approved animation. Choosing or reloading another source clears the
unchecked options; they are submission settings, not fields in the authored
contact specification.
