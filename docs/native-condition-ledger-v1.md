# Native motion row identities and exported-curve failures

`scripts/native_condition_ledger.py` attributes every native constraint row to its actor, metric, units, skin-joint/node identity and exact sample times. Native key bounds also retain the authored track and original key index. Contact rows retain point or centroid correspondence, target coordinates, and every declared game-frame rate/phase population. An unavailable speed clock remains one unavailable failed row; it is not replaced by another clock or replicated for each vertex. Actor, track, skin-joint and point order remain unchanged.

The ledger is constructed from validated `SceneContacts` and `SceneEdits` declarations. It does not run forward kinematics, measure motion, recalibrate caps, change controls or approve a clip. `compare` consumes complete saved decoded residuals and affine vectors/caps/scales, reports every decoded failure, and identifies affine false negatives. Failure is strictly above zero. Physical excess is the saved scalar residual multiplied by its normalization scale; this operation is distinct from a new measurement. Unavailable clocks have no physical measurement or units. Nonfinite observations or arithmetic and incomplete populations reject the diagnostic.

An isolated public-source copy passed **55 tests in 17.32 seconds**, including **34 new ledger checks** and the existing native norm checks. Coverage includes complete row-count/block parity, physical rate units and two/three-sample support, unsorted skin joints and track declarations, two edited actors, world/moving-object/partner contact correspondences, individual and centroid patches, all twelve rate/phase clocks, exact touch events, unavailable clocks with multiple vertices, one-ULP failures, affine false negatives, nonfinite arithmetic and independent returned metadata.
