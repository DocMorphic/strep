# Competing surface vertices in scene proposals

Development experiment, 2026-10-18. Earlier complete-clock proposals failed
when a previously nonmaximal surface point became the deepest overlap. This
study retains multiple point constraints in the local model. It produces no
accepted animation, export or change to the Studio selection.

The unchanged two-character high-five is a development case for reusable
scene correction, not a restriction on supported action prompts. All original
release requirements and the single entire-project goal remain active.

## Complete selection and derivative qualification

The study considers all **200 required or protected directed partner
conditions** in the 120-frame diagnostic. Four conditions have positive-depth
source vertices at the unchanged warm controls:

| Frame | Direction | Positive vertices retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 33 | A to B | 17 |
| 52 | B to A | 6 |
| 54 | B to A | 8 |
| 66 | A to B | 18 |

All **49 points** are retained, including the two earlier counterexamples.
There is no top-k filter. This selection does not claim coverage of outside
points that might enter a surface after a proposal. Complete nonlinear
geometry checks remain mandatory for retention.

Each query retains both original 18,056-vertex surfaces, the original 36,108
target triangles and eight skin influences. The new
[`SampledArticulatedTrack`](sampled-articulated-track-v1.md) evaluates the
needed original rotation frames while checking edit bounds over the entire
clock. Reference and finite-difference observations use the original
full-clock evaluator. World poses and depth arrays match exactly; original
strict pass masks match. All 49 point derivatives pass the predeclared 0.1%
directional check. This technical tolerance adds no physical clearance or
contact tolerance. It does not independently qualify all 480 macro geometry
derivatives in the earlier model.

## Search targets versus physical boundaries

The bundle adds 49 hard point cuts to all 496 original floor, partner,
contact, orientation and motion rows. The model has **545 rows, 72 control
columns, 504 hard rows, 24 original control-vector balls and 11 affine-vector
cones**. Original five-degree joint and two-centimetre root permissions,
protected keys, native motion ceilings and physical screens remain fixed.

The earlier policy adds a larger search reserve to already-failing required
rows. With the new bundle that affine model reports `PrimalInfeasible` at
trust sizes 0.25, 0.5 and 1. An exact change of optimization coordinates then
covers the complete original control domain and is also infeasible. This
diagnoses this affine model and its search targets; it does not prove that
physical motion is impossible.

A fresh study replaces only those adaptive proposal targets with constant
interior reserves: 0.1 mm for depth and point error, 0.1 degree for orientation
and normalized 0.0001 for motion. Every physical constraint and all 545 rows
remain. The complete-domain proposal solves and passes strict local output
checks. Optimization coordinates are translated; original source-relative
edit budgets are never rebased. The planned physical-boundary-only fallback
is not run because the constant-interior model supplies a direction.

Crucially, actual retention still uses the **unchanged original 496-row
nonlinear guard**, required/protected masks, original worst-search-progress
test and original control balls. The proposal's easier search targets do not
become easier acceptance criteria.

## Actual failures

| Fraction of proposed direction | Completed checks | Concrete rejection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full | All 16 global rows | A meeting point, hand-to-hand point, B normal, actual partner normal and B tangent |
| Half | Global rows and frames 0..24 | B floor penetration at frame 24 |
| Quarter | Global rows and frames 0..23 | B floor penetration at frame 23 |

Every trial scans the full original motion tracks for the global checks.
Geometry stops only after a measured mandatory failure. Unchecked remaining
frames are explicitly unverified; they are not reported as passing. All three
trials preserve the warm controls exactly. No candidate animation is exported.

The constant-interior proposal resolves the local solver conflict but still
does not provide a valid nonlinear step. The next correction method needs
smaller feasibility-restoration steps and refreshed linearizations, preserving
the original final gate. Repeating these same rays or loosening physical and
motion limits would not address the measured failures.

## Verification and scope

All **202 focused source checks pass locally and on Windows/Ubuntu**, including
21 new original-frame evaluation cases
([hosted run](https://github.com/DocMorphic/strep/actions/runs/39139808709)).
Serial Tensor jobs retain a 2 GiB process-tree cap, numeric jobs 512 MiB,
with the unchanged 600 MiB free-memory reserve, 15-second stable admission and
300-second execution limit. The three nonlinear jobs complete without another
memory stop. Completed and failed prior studies remain intact.

The final audit replays resource records, streams source hashes, reconstructs
bundle/search algebra, checks hosted logs and verifies actual rejection
witnesses and unchanged controls. It binds the previous whole-goal and
invariant receipts rather than rerunning ancestor experiments. Only goal
progress text changes; all release requirements remain fixed.

candidate approval. Sampled vertex penetration does not establish continuous
collision safety, edge/self-collision, forces, planted feet or realistic

human ratings and cleanup remain required. Model weights and training data
are unchanged; no training or model inference is performed in this study.
