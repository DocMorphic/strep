## Restoration trial and complete baseline window

The v2 trial adds one explicit nonnegative clearance slack to the inner optimization, so an initially infeasible collision can be reduced within a bounded step. This slack relaxes surface rows only; edit, envelope and adjacent-change limits remain hard. It uses a 30 mm numerical scale, 120 mm cap and weight 30 on the normalized slack. Every returned slack is recorded. Slack is not an acceptance tolerance: the nonlinear per-frame penetration check and unchanged final contact/collision screens still apply. Two focused tests establish partial collision improvement with a retained hard motion limit and zero final slack for a feasible target. They do not prove success on the character experiment.
