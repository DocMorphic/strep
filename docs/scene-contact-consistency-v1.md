# Necessary rigid-point contact consistency

Scene planning now reports overlapping contact pairs that cannot satisfy their declared point tolerances under ideal rigidity. Two offsets on the same actor joint have a fixed separation. When their targets share a rigid frame, the target separation is also fixed: this covers two world points, points on one object, or offsets on one partner joint. The reverse triangle inequality requires the separation difference to be no larger than the sum of the two contact tolerances.

The check uses exact rational values of supplied integer/binary-float coordinates and a strict squared inequality, avoiding rounded square roots at tolerance boundaries. Reports retain contact indices/IDs, the complete inclusive overlap interval, target frame, both squared distances and summed tolerance as numerator/denominator witnesses. Their assumptions are ideal rigid transforms and point-ball tolerances, not inferred anatomy. A contradiction concerns those authored point requirements; it does not establish that the broader action or interaction is impossible under different targets.

Every same-actor/same-joint pair is counted; disjoint pairs are counted separately. Every overlapping pair is retained as contradicted, not contradicted by this necessary condition, or unassessed with a reason. Mesh/region contacts are never replaced by joint proxies. Target points in different rigid frames require world pose tracks and remain unassessed. Ordinary annotations do not hide declared point requirements. No contradiction is not feasibility: joint reach, single-contact self-relations, normals, body geometry, collision, dynamics and model accuracy remain unchecked.

Metadata diagnostics accept up to 4,096 complete same-joint pairs; larger populations reject explicitly without thinning. This is a diagnostic computation budget, not a physical limit or animation-category whitelist. Authored contact intervals, tolerances and game profiles are unchanged.

The Studio plan check and metadata-only guide CLI retain this report. Studio displays conflicting contact IDs and overlap frames, and says to revise before preparation; downloads retain failed intent. `scene_generation.py prepare` saves contradictory scenes/plans, the audit and diagnostic source snapshot with a rejected pipeline status before pose compilation, model loading or geometry queries. The diagnostic-target option does not bypass an exact metadata contradiction. Valid preparations bind `contact-consistency.json` and its method snapshot. Pre-inference/assembly checks recompute the report and reject missing, changed, rebound or conflicting audits. Historical batches without these artifacts remain supported within their existing contracts.
