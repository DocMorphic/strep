# Hand references and complete contact geometry

These are development studies at 2 s on the configured two-actor scene, not
new neural generation, complete animations, default changes or release
evidence. Source clips, skin weights, translations, scales and original-zero
bound references remain unchanged. The self-surface baseline and its exact
witnesses are recorded in [native-self-surface-v1.md](native-self-surface-v1.md).

## Reference posture versus contact

Moving finger quaternions halfway toward the stored bind posture clears the
detected right-hand self-crossings within the prior 20-degree finger/30-degree
Thumb1 caps and 60-mm displacement allowance. An arm/wrist-only fit then passes
all 210 pose inequalities but produces 1,056 proper partner crossings. Thus
marker alignment and an individually nonfolding hand do not establish valid
contact between two complete hand surfaces.

A second explicit geometric reference minimally aligns outgoing finger bones
toward metacarpal directions projected into a declared palm plane. It preserves
sampled affine chains and scales, clips rotations relative to the original
source, and measures actual skin afterward. The first version aligns joints
2–4 and leaves metacarpal joint 1 at the halfway bind reference. This is a
geometric posture, not an inferred anatomical range or a universally open hand.

## Supporting material frames

A separate screen evaluates reference-pose material frames in the declared
45-mm-radius palm region, with normals within 45 degrees of the existing hint.
For every candidate it queries all vertices in the complete positive-weight
hand-triangle population. A frame qualifies only when no such vertex protrudes
more than 1 mm beyond the material-origin plane along its winding normal.
Among eligible frames, selection uses proximity to the existing probe, without
using fitted benchmark outcomes or anatomical labels.

A has 162 valid candidate frames and B 158; three qualify per actor. Both
select vertex 17039. Selected protrusion is 0.513928 mm for A and 0.543690 mm
for B; probe distances are 32.496/32.615 mm. These are support-compatible
patches within the declared region, not a claim that they are the anatomical
center of the palm. Human review remains pending.

28 observations replayed. A preceding script-typo failure is retained and
resource-replayed separately: 17.250 s, 70,893,568 bytes, 19 observations.

and 80 for B. Exact rational witnesses confirm all 211 represented-coordinate
