## Inspect the selected surface direction

Region previews now include front, side and top SVG views of **every selected triangle and vertex**, with direction arrows from each triangle's original winding. The views use the character's default pose in metres, independent of the current motion-preview time and scene placement. Local vertex numbers match the saved region's explicit correspondence indices. A direction along the viewing axis appears as a dot toward the viewer or a cross away; it is not silently flipped to face the camera. Distinct source triangles that share corners remain separate, including opposed winding. Directions are not inferred anatomical palms, outward normals or approved contact targets.

A separate source-bound `orientation.json` retains original face references, local corner indices, per-triangle centres, unit winding normals and twice-areas, the area-weighted centre, and existing patch winding diagnostics. Its hash is included in the preview result and saved authoring receipt. Save verifies the complete file population and replays the entire orientation record against the same source mesh and ownership selection. Refreshing hashes cannot make changed directions or corners pass that replay. The portable bundle's existing contents and verifier remain unchanged.

The browser checks complete geometry and direction consistency before drawing the three fitted views. New previews and changed profiles, draft/side/picks or settings clear the old drawing; saving still leaves the separate bundle for explicit correspondence and preview/apply.
