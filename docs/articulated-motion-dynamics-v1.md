# Explicit body-tree joint-demand diagnostic

`scripts/articulated_motion_dynamics.py` computes internal joint forces and moments for an explicitly supplied rigid-body tree and baked motion. This is a numerical component, not an inferred human body model or a strength-stat calibration. It currently exposes a Python function; supplied-rig physical-profile conversion, contact-force allocation and Studio integration remain separate work.

Each body needs a unique ID, parent ID, positive mass, physically valid inertia about its COM, local joint anchors on both connected bodies and provenance. The root floats and has no parent anchor. Body order need not be topological; multiple roots, missing parents and cycles are rejected. Every pose supplies world COM positions and body rotations on one explicit uniform clock, plus a complete external force and torque-about-COM population. Missing ground or object reactions are never inferred as zero from a preview. A supplied zero is an explicit modeling assumption.

Original anchors must stay connected within the declared numerical accuracy at every sample, including endpoints. Disconnected tracks are rejected rather than repaired silently. The existing rigid-body derivative diagnostic supplies each body's translational/angular demand. The new recursion transports every child joint wrench to its parent's joint center, including the lever-arm moment. A separate direct aggregation checks the whole-tree force and moment identity. The floating root's residual must vanish under the declared external wrenches for the necessary sampled root-balance condition to pass.

Internal joint moments remain visible even when root balance passes. They do not imply that a person can produce them: calibrated torque capacities, axis restrictions, contact allocation, support balance, impacts and anatomy remain absent. The distinction between inertial demand, contact forces and actuation is consistent with [MuJoCo's articulated equations and inverse dynamics](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/latest/computation/index.html#general-framework); this component implements its own rigid-tree wrench recursion and does not call MuJoCo.

Three-point derivatives retain every interior sample. Cross-phase stencils and unknown support phases are explicitly unassessed; endpoints have no derivative estimate. An empty assessed population cannot pass by omission. Numeric input/operation limits bound bodies, frames, clip duration, coordinates, rotations, forces and complete joint demands. The floating-point derivative and wrench identities are diagnostics, not continuous-time physical certificates.

Cases cover a horizontal two-link chain's known gravity loads, an explicit root-balancing external wrench with nonzero internal torque, rotation/translation covariance, reversed body ordering, constant nonprincipal spin and phase/boundary accounting. Rejection fixtures cover inconsistent topology/anchors, nonphysical properties and missing/nonfinite data. No human clip receives a body profile or approval from these synthetic cases.
