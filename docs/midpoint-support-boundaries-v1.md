# Between-frame floor constraints in coupled support correction

Development experiment, 2026-09-28. The project-wide release goal remains active.

The preceding coupled leg/root experiment improved six of eleven targeted clips. Inspection of all eleven solver logs found 108 trial steps that passed their key-frame constraints but were rejected by the interpolated floor guard. All eleven selected clips still had a foot-peak regression relative to raw. This is evidence of a missing constraint in the proposal model, not proof that the current edits are infeasible.

`scripts/midpoint_support_block.py` adds the actual skin floor at every affected half-frame to the conic proposal constraints. Values use the existing serialized-pose evaluator, including float32 key-time drift. The smooth derivative interpolates local rotations along the short rotation arc, differentiates the small local interpolation map, and propagates its axes analytically through every weighted skin descendant. Each row touches only its two adjacent editable poses. Root offset derivatives account for endpoint and midpoint parent orientations; editable root ancestors are explicitly unsupported by this derivative and fail early.

The independent nonlinear midpoint guard, full exported-file audit, original raw-relative edit budgets, source floor preservation, support anchors/heights, acceleration, rotation steps and boundary-speed tolerances are unchanged. The method does not interpolate world-space vertices or assume that endpoint floor checks bound the intervening trajectory. Checking half-frames still does not certify continuous-time collision freedom.

Five tests pass in 22.86 seconds: the shortest rotation arc near the wrap boundary, real smooth derivative and serialized-source feasibility checks on three rigs spanning jump, dance and kneel-rise, sparse adjacent-frame isolation, and a synthetic rotating link whose endpoints clear the floor while its interpolated midpoint penetrates by 0.5 m. The midpoint row supplies the expected root correction derivatives. The source rejection diagnostic is retained at `reports/midpoint-support-preflight-v1/source-failures.json`.
