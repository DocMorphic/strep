# Full-clock constrained scene proposals

Development experiment, 2026-10-18. The full saved-clock audit showed that
contact-frame improvement lost a passing directed condition elsewhere. This
experiment builds a complete editor-clock model and rejects proposals against
individual conditions. No animation is approved or exported by this study.

## Model and unchanged permissions

The original native high-five pair, seeds 77/88, is unchanged. The diagnostic
warm seed is the previous unselected contact-vector correction. Its 72 controls
remain relative to the original clips, with five-degree budgets for eleven
selected joints per actor inside frames 30..90 and two-centimetre constant
root translations over all 120 frames. Protected joint keys stay protected.

Six serial Tensor jobs rebuild every original integer frame. Each frame has
two floor penetration rows and both directed partner rows: **480 geometry
rows**, retaining all 18,056 vertices, eight skin influences and 36,108 target
triangles. Every row has 72 control derivatives. The minimum-distance
optimization field and original auditor values remain separate. The original
saved-file measurements supply required passing masks; they do not replace
editor values or derivatives.

Three point contacts, three normal conditions, two tangents and eight complete
skin/joint speed/acceleration tracks add sixteen rows, for **496 original
conditions**. The local solve has 24 original control balls, 11 affine vector
cones, 449 explicitly required rows and 455 hard linear rows after including
the warm seed's other passing conditions. Local vector cones cover the actual
paired point offsets and current motion-peak vectors; they do not replace
complete nonlinear motion checks.

All floor screens and native motion ceilings must pass before retention.
Original observed passing conditions must be restored or preserved, including
the lost A-to-B condition at frame 66. The other passing warm-seed conditions
remain protected. A retained step would also need a strictly lower worst
search violation inside every original control ball.

Physical development screens remain 5 mm penetration, 30 mm point error and
15-degree orientation error. Search targets sit 0.1 mm inside depth/point
screens, 0.1 degree inside orientation screens and normalized 0.0001 inside
motion ceilings. For a required row already failing, its declared search
target is `max(base, -initial_auditor_margin + base)`. This targets repair
without changing the original physical boundary. Trust is 0.25 in the original
normalized coordinates. No author permission, reference or failed result is
rebased.

## Motion memory and derivative checks

The first full-array global derivative job stopped at the 600 MiB available-
memory guard before producing a Jacobian. Its observed process peak was
1,298,780,160 bytes, below the separate 2 GiB guard. That failed job remains
intact. Neither guard nor motion threshold was lowered to continue.

The replacement scans every frame and every original vertex/joint without
retaining those gradients, then differentiates the active original skin rows.
Every evaluation repeats the complete scan. All 119 velocity and 118
acceleration intervals per track remain included. The canonical version uses
the original finite-difference definitions and exactly matches the full-array
evaluator's measured scalar values, vector components, peak identities and
strict pass/fail masks at the warm seed. Its observed peak, including that
reference comparison, is 1,042,575,360 bytes.

All sixteen global scalar rows and 33 vector components pass the predeclared
0.1% directional derivative check. Those are technical checks, not added
physical tolerances. The 480 geometry rows have analytic current-feature
derivatives; an independent directional check of every geometry row was not
performed. Solver status is never treated as nonlinear feasibility.

## Competing surface witnesses

At frame 33, vertex 17005 moves from 3.803640 to 5.343897 mm penetration in
the base half step. The current-maximum model predicts a passing frame depth
of 4.522878 mm. The competing vertex was already inside at the warm seed and
has a nonzero control derivative; this example does not demonstrate a flat
outside-penetration gradient.

Adding that vertex's derivative as a hard model cut retains all 496 original
rows and their limits. Its old-direction predicted margins are negative for
both full and half steps, so the cut changes the direction. The new full step
passes global contact/motion checks and the earlier frame-33 failure, but it
still fails the original required condition at frame 66.

The second replay finds two violating vertices already inside at the warm
seed. The worst is vertex 16955, moving from 4.617657 to 6.419126 mm in the
witness-aware half step. That frame's scalar model instead predicts a passing
4.950000 mm depth. A different initially nonmaximal vertex becomes critical.
One additional cut is therefore insufficient. The next local model needs a
retained bundle of competing positive-depth vertex constraints for mandatory
frame/direction conditions, while preserving complete nonlinear checks.
Repeating the same fractions or waiving acceleration caps is not justified.
