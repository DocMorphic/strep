# Individual running controls — frozen protocol

2026-09-24, before new outputs were inspected. Goal: measurable authoring controls, not inferred strength or fitness.

Configuration: `benchmarks/control-calibration-v1.json`. Two controls, three levels, seeds 11/22 for development and 55 reserved for evaluation. Seven prompts (six conditions plus neutral) give 21 text-conditioned clips. Path, checkpoint, duration, guidance, correction settings and rig match the previous study. No prompt or threshold tuning after held-out inspection.

Arm swing targets are 20/45/70 degrees of mean bilateral p95–p5 sagittal shoulder-to-wrist angle span. Torso targets are 0/10/20 degrees median sagittal Hips-to-Neck1 forward lean. Text adjectives are ordinal requests: the model receives no numerical angle channel. Direct edits target the numerical descriptors and retain each take's timing. All angles are source-skeleton measurements; transferred target-rig values are measured separately.

Kimodo recommends concise behavior descriptions, warns that text/constraints can conflict, and supports dense root paths. This motivates fixed paths and changing one descriptive feature per prompt. [Official best practices](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/kimodo/docs/key_concepts/limitations.html). Its end-effector constraints are not arbitrary continuous style sliders; local rotations supplied for full-body constraints are converted into position constraints. [Constraint specification](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/kimodo/docs/user_guide/constraints.html).

Use the unchanged loop and stance pipeline for all generated text takes. Deterministic edits operate **after** cleanup on the matched neutral loop: rotate shoulder subtrees to scale the arm-angle excursion about its median, or apply a constant spine offset fitted to the torso target. Preserve root, legs, contacts, sample times and source motion. This ordering avoids asking subsequent loop selection to preserve an edit; it is not evidence that arbitrary processing after editing is harmless. Record both the pre-edit corrected baseline and edited result. No simulated strength, load or balance dynamics is claimed.

Frozen engineering screens: source target errors at most 2 degrees arm / 1 degree torso; adjacent response gaps at least 10 / 5 degrees; target-rig response-delta error against baseline at most 5 degrees; unintended change in the other angle descriptor at most 3 degrees; unchanged root/lower-body positions within 1e-5 m; local edit at most 35 degrees. Retain existing loop, speed/path and contact-regression checks. Per-level release requires every development and held-out take to pass; control ordering and target preservation must also pass. Failed conditions remain visible. These are pilot tolerances, not biological limits or professional animation standards.

Report the complete grid, full raw versus selected/processed text responses, within-seed ordering, target error, speed, contact quality, unwanted changes, export round trips and target-rig measurements. Include inherited defects rather than granting direct edits automatic acceptance. Keep numerical control accuracy distinct from human naturalness, self-collision, balance and engine import; those are unassessed. A single held-out seed is an engineering check, not a population generalization claim.

The authoring mapping may expose tested angle choices and store capability metadata. It must not equate agility with an arm angle or pretend endurance changes a short fresh run. Game-specific stat-to-style rules remain explicit design hypotheses until actions that reveal those capabilities are tested.
