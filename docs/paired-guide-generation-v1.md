The unchanged checkpoint now has a fixed comparison against the five existing high-five seeds: 1301, 2089, 3253, 4099 and 5101. Each of two guide methods generates both actors, giving 20 new raw clips and ten pairs. Both methods constrain full-body poses at frames 0 and 149. At frame 75, one constrains the left hand and the other the full body. Prompts, five-second duration, seed, 100 diffusion steps, separated CFG weights [2,2] and disabled postprocessing stay fixed. The original ten baseline actor clips are retained.

The common pose fixture comes from the previously inspected seed-1301 region-fit export. It is reused for every seed, with its saved scene placement and no result-specific repositioning. Its event frame has zero measured vertex penetration but fails distributed-region contact; it is not validated ground truth. This is a development experiment on known prompts and seeds. It cannot serve as held-out validation. The actual Kimodo constraint channels use SOMA30; supplied SOMA77 fingers and visible skin contact are not constrained.

Exact SOMA77-to30-to77 conversion preserves wrist positions within 0.0004 mm and leaves the palm gap at 42.239 mm. Exact skeletal adherence alone therefore cannot pass the original 30 mm palm-point screen. This does not excuse the generated errors; it separates target inconsistency from sampling error.

These target-planning normals are a distinct diagnostic from the generation comparison's 15-degree point-normal screen.

## Conservative collision-query prefilter

`convex_partner_surface.py` rejects query vertices strictly outside an expanded convex hull of the target's vertices. All target triangles lie within that hull. Retained points use the existing Trimesh signed-distance calculation in batches of at most 32. Each plane is expanded to include every original vertex plus a numerical margin; degenerate hull construction falls back to the original axis-aligned candidate set. The hull itself is never treated as the colliding surface.

Three focused tests pass, covering rotated thin shapes, disconnected closed components, translations, original vertex indexing, bounded batches and degeneracy. Independent replay of the old complete 61-sample, two-variant audit matches all 244 directional penetration depths, counts and deepest vertex IDs. Queries fall from 199,216 to 29,978, an 84.95% reduction. The replay took 185.54 seconds on the shared laptop; this is not an isolated speed benchmark. Existing running studies keep their original frozen implementation. None of these checks certifies triangle-only crossings, continuous time, anatomy, self-collision or motion quality.
