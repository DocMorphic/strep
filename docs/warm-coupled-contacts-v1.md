# Checked proposals and warm contact refinement

`scripts/checked_native_proposal.py` wraps the unchanged bounded conic solver. A solver status alone cannot authorize a proposal. The wrapper projects each explicit backoff into the original control boxes, respects the exact trust fraction despite floating addition, and recomputes every affine norm row. All protected residuals must be nonpositive, without added tolerance; the positive soft maximum cannot increase and its squared sum must improve. Nonfinite arithmetic, an infeasible affine anchor, and a zero or stalled step cannot pass.

The saved earlier repair motivates this gate: clipping its tiny trust-box overshoot still left nine protected affine failures. This is a diagnostic for that saved model, not a proof that contact repair is physically impossible. The new wrapper evaluates the solver's selected direction; it does not search all feasible conic directions or recover an alternative from the primary phase if the secondary phase fails the strict check.

`scripts/warm_coupled_contacts.py` rebuilds the central model at each accepted, decoded start. `--start-controls` remains relative to the original source curves, and all source-rate caps, edit permissions and contact guards remain unchanged. Final retention compares with the serialized start, rather than crediting an improvement that existed before this run. A timeout, rejected trial or failed final geometry audit preserves that start's controls and exported files. Provisional clips remain archived separately. The caller is responsible for supplying a previously validated baseline; preserving it does not itself perform or claim a new geometry audit.

Checked affine steps are proposals. Exported Float32 curves must still pass the complete native conditions, individual contact guards and edit audit. Further backoffs are checked through exported motion. A further improvement requires the complete declared sampled geometry audit before retention. Neither this affine wrapper nor warm refinement grants motion quality, engine import, continuous/self collision, human review or release approval.

Validation includes exact-bound and full-row failures, dense/sparse parity, a real small CPU conic solve, actual serialized curves, reconstruction of complete tiny geometry observations, warm fallback, original source guards and worker/output exclusion. Fixture proposals injected into retention tests exercise the downstream gate; they are not model-generated motion. Production motion and the previously completed import/replay studies have not been rerun for this source change. All release evidence arrays remain empty, and real human ratings and timed cleanup records remain zero.
