## Completion contract

The machine-readable release matrix is `benchmarks/project-release-v1.json`. Every required capability must have working end-to-end evidence. Human/semantic, geometry/contact, transition, transfer, export and offline-install checks are independent gates; passing numerical checks cannot substitute for human review. Missing evidence stays missing. Freeze detailed test fixtures and thresholds before any held-out release run, and preserve all failures and raw outputs.

Release requires broad held-out evaluation across actions, seeds, proportions/rigs, objects and actor pairs; successful import and playback in a real game engine; reproducible project/asset packages; blind independent animation review and measured cleanup time. Define and disclose the supported operating range. Never claim universal coverage, calibrated physical traits, physics certification or commercial production readiness from the current small studies.

## Work sequence within this single goal

1. Editable support intervals and spatial targets; hard pose/root edit budgets; independent post-validation with rejection of failed candidates. Preserve existing baselines.
2. Scene representation and contact tools: object geometry, grip targets, attachment/release, sitting/leaning, collision checks; reusable across actions.
3. Two-actor timelines, shared targets, contact synchronization and reactions. Distinguish generated motion from deterministic corrections.
4. Rig import/mapping/rest alignment and target-mesh-aware correction across different proportions; game-engine import and root/event checks.
5. Existing-clip editing, sequence/transition/loop authoring and measurable style controls; calibrate game-stat mappings instead of inventing physical equivalences.
6. Broad generation and editing study with held-out prompts and compositional cases. Research alternative models or fine-tuning only against measured shortcomings, with licensed data and local feasibility checks.
7. Complete offline product integration, reproducible installation, animator validation, cleanup-time study and release documentation. Revisit failures until the declared release gates are met.

Work may interleave when it improves coverage or avoids overfitting one action. Do not get stuck polishing running/get-ups while object/partner, rig and engine gaps remain unimplemented. End-of-turn summaries are progress reports; they do not complete or pause this project-wide goal.
