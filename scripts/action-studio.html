    Local pose adjustments
 
    Rotate a bone around a source frame, with a smooth return to the original pose. Preview shows the source until you apply the edit.

    Bone pairs
    
 
    Review floor clearance and action after mirroring. Scene targets need re-authoring and gameplay markers need review. Existing loop playback is exported as a finite clip.
    
 
 
    
 
    
 
    World-space joint targets
 
    Move a hand, foot or another joint toward an exact world pose. This creates a separate candidate while keeping the clip's timing.
    
 
 
    
 
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    Regenerate a section from a description
 
    Use the first and last source frames above. Describe the replacement action for that section. This keeps the clip's duration and surrounding motion; speed and local pose adjustments are separate edits.
    
 
 
    
 
    
 
 
    

    

   
    
 
    Gameplay markers
    
 
    
 
    Markers describe your intended game timing. They do not prove a foot plant, hit or grasp. Blended events need explicit timing review before the runtime emits them.
    
 
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    
 

    

   
    
 
    Join clips
    
 
    
 
 
    
 
 
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    The next clip moves horizontally to the first clip's overlap start. Height stays unchanged. The heading adjustment is explicit; no facing direction is guessed.
    
 
    Source contact targets stay in the review file. Blending may break support or change the action; inspect the join before using it. This creates a finite clip, not a certified loop.
    
 

    

   
    
 
    Make a loop
    
 
    
 
 
 
    
 
    
 
    
 
    Find candidate cycles
  
    Search a frame grid using the root mode, blend and turn above. Results compare movement continuity; review the action itself before choosing.
    
  
    
  
    
  
  
  
    
  
    
 
    
 
    Choose a repeating action and leave blend frames after the cycle. The cycle begins with the source's ending continuation, then blends back into the selected motion. The first two samples preserve the original seam movement.
    
 
    Travel mode accumulates source displacement; in-place mode removes net cycle travel. The explicit turn still accumulates. Review three cycles for foot sliding, ground penetration and abrupt changes; a closed seam alone does not establish a natural loop.

    readme.txt
    Welcome to strep OS
    A desktop for motion.
    01 / Describe it
    Open New motion, write your own action and add timed steps. Examples are a starting point, never a fixed action list.
    02 / Make it yours
    Browse takes, scrub through a clip and edit its request to generate variations. Download GLB, BVH or a complete animation pack.
    03 / Review the details
    Use Inspector for contact and transition diagnostics. Objects, partner interactions, rig transfer and calibrated character stats are still in development.
    Your workspace
    Drag title bars, resize from the bottom corner, or use the red, yellow and green window controls. Reopen apps from the dock. Double-click a title bar to fill the desktop. Yellow minimizes into the Dock; green enters full screen. Hover green for tiling options. Click the wallpaper to reveal your desktop, and again to bring the windows back.
    Ctrl + Alt + Space Spotlight   Ctrl + ↑ Mission Control
    Ctrl + Alt + Tab App switcher   Esc Back
    Ctrl + Alt + N New motion   Ctrl + Alt + L Library
    Your saved animations are available in My animations. Use Preview to compare takes and download editable clips.
