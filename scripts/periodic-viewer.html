What these controls mean

These sliders set measured motion angles. They do not assign agility, strength or stamina. Three development takes and one newly generated test take were evaluated. One new seed is limited evidence, not broad reliability.

A passed motion check does not establish naturalness, balance, body collisions or game-engine compatibility. Larger arm swings can introduce a visible hitch at the loop boundary. All failed variants remain available to inspect. The original motion files are preserved.
